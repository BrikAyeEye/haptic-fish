01 // The Philosophy

Overcoming Touchscreen Fatigue

The Glass Screen Has Hit Its Limit.

When every museum exhibit, brand experience, and digital tool is trapped behind identical sheets of flat tempered glass, visitor attention evaporates. Empirical research confirms that purely digital media suffer from a Digital Devaluation Penalty—users subconsciously value digital assets 11% to 22% less due to a total absence of tangible ownership.

At Haptic Fish, we practice Physical-First Computing: heavy brass dials with fluid dampening, multi-axis optical tracking spheres, continuous thermal printing, and cast silicone feedback bodies that trigger real proprioceptive memory.

Museum Artifact • Historical Re-Engineering

Der mechatronische Diät-Astrolab (1540 // Modern)

Re-engineering of a 500-year-old computational masterpiece from the Astronomicum Caesareum (Peter Apian, 1540, SLUB Dresden). Designed as an “accessible mechanical proxy” for the Mathematisch-Physikalischer Salon (Zwinger Dresden): concentric ball-bearing brass rings, optical rotary sensors, RP2040 microcontroller, and an industrial thermal dispatch unit that prints personalized historical court diet & scandal protocols based on the legendary 1730 Zeithain encampment of Augustus the Strong.

Somatic Tech • Screen-Free Wellbeing

Somatic Memory Orb

A screen-free, autonomous biofeedback and stress regulation device housed in a seamless cast silicone chassis with wireless Qi induction charging. Incorporates analog Force-Sensing Resistor (FSR) circuitry and an onboard EEPROM interaction memory. The system monitors pressure duration and frequency, dynamically dampening resonant vibration cycles (LRA actuators) to induce somatic calm during acute cognitive overload.

04 // Engineering Stack

The Physical Computing Pipeline

Tools, microcontrollers, and precision fabrication techniques deployed in our Dresden laboratory.

Dual-core Arm Cortex M33/M0+, PIO state machines, real-time interrupt handling.
PixArt PMW3360
12,000 CPI optical flow sensor over SPI for micrometer-accurate tactile surface tracking.
Thermal Printing
Direct line thermal printheads for instant tactile receipts, protocols, and generative dispatches.
Parametric CAD
Code-driven OpenSCAD and Autodesk Fusion 360 models for rapid millimeter-accurate iterations.
Cold-Casting & Metal
Bronze, brass, and copper cold-casting with genuine metal feel, patina, and tactile heft.
